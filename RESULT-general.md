# Rusty numbers a = (1,d): toward all d

Setup (Fokkink–Joshi, arXiv 2506.13337, §4): A_n = B_{2n−1} + d·B_{2n}, where B is the complement of A, taken blockwise by mex. Then τ = d+1 and κ = 2d+3. Conjecture: A_n − κn is (d+1)-automatic.

## 2. Structure found (computational, not proved)

`d(n) = A_{n+1} − κ(n+1)` is generated by a **k-block (d+1)-uniform substitution**: a window of k consecutive values d(n..n+k−1) determines the block d(qn+c .. qn+c+q+k−2), where q = d+1, for a fixed shift c. Data: `pairs_1_30.txt`, `ktuple.txt`; scripts `pair2.py`, `ktuple.py`.

| d | minimal k | shift c | window-letters | checked n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–9 | 1 | 0–2 | ≤ 18 | ≥ 8·10⁵ |
| 10–13 | 2 | 3 | 47–60 | ≥ 5.7·10⁵ |
| 14, 15 | 3 | 4 | 87, 97 | ≥ 5·10⁵ |
| 20 | 5 | 6 | 191 | 3.8·10⁵ |
| 25, 30 | 6 | 7 | 272, 307 | 2.6–3·10⁵ |

The range of d(n) grows roughly linearly, about [−1.3d, 0.57d] (`pairs_1_30.txt`). It stays bounded for every d tested up to 8·10⁶ terms, which is the weak form of the conjecture. A k-block uniform substitution on a finite alphabet produces an automatic sequence (Cobham), so this structure, if it holds, implies the conjecture for that d.

**Why one uniform argument is hard.** Each A_j "seeds" the q anti-recurrences near index qj. The window that the A's fall into has length about κ, but consecutive A gaps vary by up to the range width (about 1.9d), so for large d several earlier A's can affect one block. Hence k grows with d (k = 1 up to d = 9, then 2, 3, …). This is also why Fokkink–Joshi's A₁-boundedness (one A per interval of length κ) fails for d ≥ 3, and why d = 4 shows the n = 348 delay.

## 3. Precise conjecture (open)

For every d ≥ 1 there are k = k(d) and c = c(d) such that d(n) is a fixed point of a k-block (d+1)-uniform substitution on a finite alphabet. In particular A_n − (2d+3)n is (d+1)-automatic. Its values lie in a range of width O(d).

## 4. Not done

- **A hand proof for all d.** The missing ingredients are (i) boundedness of A_n − κn for all d (the weak conjecture is itself unproved for rusty numbers) and (ii) a locality lemma showing that a k(d)-window of earlier A's determines each block.
- **Walnut for d = 11** needs more than ~1 GB of heap (the user's memory cap). Rerunning with e.g. `-Xmx4g` would likely finish.
- **Walnut for d = 9, 10, 12 and beyond.** The msd Myhill–Nerode inference (`../infer_msd.py`) needed prefixes beyond 10⁸ terms (some states are first reached very late, like the n = 348 effect). The substitution in §2 could generate longer exact data or build the automaton directly. Not attempted.
- No external check.
